# Dataset selection and provenance

## Decision

Use all published yellow taxi trip records for January–March 2025, together with the official TLC taxi-zone lookup and boundary shapefile. The download files, measured byte sizes and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `data/processed/quality.json`. Actual row counts, coverage, missingness, filtering and monthly summaries are generated by the pipeline rather than assumed. One record is a vendor-reported trip, not a person or a complete measure of city travel. The selected months provide millions of rows and a contiguous period for chronological evaluation. They do not establish year-round seasonality.

TLC says taxi data is supplied by authorized technology providers, makes no representation about its accuracy, and normally publishes monthly files with an approximately two-month delay. Parquet supports column projection and compressed scans. The official dictionary identifies pickup and drop-off timestamps, zone IDs, and distance in miles. The zone files provide spatial granularity, not exact trip coordinates. Historic TLC user guide update cadence and CSV statements are superseded by the current landing page.

- TLC landing page and direct monthly links: https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page
- January, February and March files use `https://d37ci6vzurychx.cloudfront.net/trip-data/yellow_tripdata_2025-MM.parquet`.
- Yellow dictionary: https://www.nyc.gov/assets/tlc/downloads/pdf/data_dictionary_trip_records_yellow.pdf
- User guide: https://www.nyc.gov/assets/tlc/downloads/pdf/trip_record_user_guide.pdf
- Zone lookup: https://d37ci6vzurychx.cloudfront.net/misc/taxi_zone_lookup.csv
- Official boundary archive: https://d37ci6vzurychx.cloudfront.net/misc/taxi_zones.zip

## Alternatives

Green and high-volume for-hire vehicle records are official and useful for broader service comparisons, but differences in operating areas, reporting and schema would require a distinct harmonization and coverage study. NOAA weather could add contextual hypotheses, but station choice, observation completeness and local-time alignment must be validated; it is therefore omitted. NYC Open Data offers related transport data but a join must have a defensible temporal and geographic key. None is necessary to establish the yellow-taxi patterns studied here.

## Conditions and caveats

Public availability is not a blanket warranty or a claim of a particular open license. Consult the source pages and NYC terms before redistribution or reuse; attribution to NYC TLC is appropriate. NYC terms: https://www.nyc.gov/home/terms-of-use.page. TLC zones include unknown/outside categories and some zones do not have ordinary polygons. The analysis excludes invalid/unknown location IDs and implausible trip characteristics; these decisions bias the retained sample. Metered pickups reflect availability, demand, service rules and reporting; they cannot alone identify causal effects, population-normalized travel rates or complete traffic.
